06 — FAQ

Questions clients ask before hiring.

Still unsure? Send a message or ask on WhatsApp.

How do we start a project?

Send a short brief through the form or WhatsApp: what the video is for, where it will be published, a reference you like and your deadline. You'll get a clear quote, timeline and the best way to send footage.

What is your typical turnaround?

Short-form reels usually take 24–72 hours, commercials 3–7 days and multi-cam event films about a week. Rush 48-hour delivery is available for launches and campaigns.

How many revisions are included?

Every project includes structured revision rounds with time-stamped feedback, so changes are fast and precise. The goal is simple: you approve a cut you're proud to publish.

How do I send large footage files?

Google Drive, Dropbox, WeTransfer or Frame.io all work. For 6K/RAW projects, lightweight proxies keep editing fast regardless of distance.

Do you work with clients outside Egypt?

Yes — most clients are remote, across Saudi Arabia, the UAE and Canada. Communication is in Arabic or English, and time zones are planned into the schedule.

Which file formats do you deliver?

H.264/H.265 for social, ProRes 422 HQ or DNxHR for broadcast, in 9:16, 16:9, 1:1 or 4:5 — plus SRT subtitles and project files on request.

How is payment handled?

Through trusted platforms such as Upwork or Mostaql, or directly by agreement. Larger projects are split into milestones so you only pay as work is approved.
